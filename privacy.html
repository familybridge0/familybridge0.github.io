Privacy Policy - Family Chat Bridge

Last updated: 28 October 2026

What the app does

Family Chat Bridge is a private tool operated by its owner for one family. With the owner's permission, it uses Google APIs to read and write messages in Google Chat, to send and manage email in Gmail, and to store files that the app itself creates in Google Drive. It uses these to relay messages between a family WhatsApp group and Google Chat, and to email conversation summaries to family members.

Data the app accesses

  • Google Chat messages and spaces of the owner's accounts.
  • Gmail: the app sends email, and reads and labels messages that family members send to it.
  • Google Drive: only files created by the app.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to provide the features described above. It is processed on a private server controlled by the owner. Message content is kept only for a limited period, as needed for summaries, and is then deleted. Access tokens are stored only on that server.

Sharing

We do not sell Google user data, do not share it with third parties, and do not use it for advertising. Some features may use a third-party processing service (for example, speech-to-text for audio messages); data is sent to such a service only to provide that feature.

Revoking access and deletion

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, and can delete the stored data from the private server.
